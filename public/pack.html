A Nightcap at Ravensmere

Before your guests arrive

  1. Print this pack. Cut out the place cards and fold them so the names stand up on the table.
  2. Cut out the regimental photograph on page 5, fold it so the handwriting is on the back, and seal it in an envelope without reading the back. (No peeking. Cecil will know.) Stick the "Envelope Two" label on the front.
  3. Put the menu and the house plan in the middle of the table. Don't open Envelope Two until Cecil asks.
  4. On the laptop, run Cecil and open the shared screen. Tick "We've printed the evidence pack" in the lobby.
  5. Everyone scans the QR code with their phone. Cecil does the rest.

How the evening works

Everyone plays a guest at Ravensmere Hall. Your phone is private: it holds your character, your secrets and anything Cecil whispers to you. The big screen is shared: Cecil narrates, lays out evidence and calls you back together. One of you is the murderer. Nobody else knows who, not even the host, because there isn't one. Cecil knows everything. Humans get to play.
