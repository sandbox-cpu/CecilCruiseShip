Before your guests come aboard

  1. Print this pack. Cut out the place cards and fold them so the names stand up on the table.
  2. Cut out the telegram on page 5 and fold the bottom strip under along the dashed line, without reading it. Put it in an envelope and stick the "Purser's Bureau" label on the front. (No peeking. Cecil will know.)
  3. Put Today's Programme and the deck plan in the middle of the table. Don't open the telegram until Cecil asks.
  4. On the laptop, run Cecil, choose Dead Reckoning in the lobby and tick "We've printed the evidence pack".
  5. Everyone scans the QR code with their phone. Cecil does the rest.

How the evening works

Everyone plays someone aboard the SS Halcyon. Your phone is private: it holds your character, your secrets and anything Cecil whispers to you. The big screen is shared: Cecil narrates, lays out evidence and calls you back together. One of you is the murderer. Nobody else knows who, not even the host, because there isn't one. Cecil knows everything. Humans get to play.
